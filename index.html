What is Vertical Farming?

Vertical farming is a modern agricultural technique in which plants are grown in vertically stacked layers instead of traditional horizontal fields.


It can be practised inside buildings, greenhouses and specially designed farming facilities. Technologies such as LED lights, hydroponics and automated monitoring help manage plant growth.

Vertical Farming Methods

Explore three modern ways of growing plants.

Hydroponics

Plants grow without soil, using water containing the essential nutrients they need.

Aeroponics

Plant roots receive water and nutrients through a fine mist in a controlled system.

Container Farming

Specially designed containers provide a controlled environment for growing crops in limited spaces.
